# Pre-final design decisions

The user rejected the initial editorial ivory/green direction as too generic. The revised identity uses platinum white, graphite, a small blue accent for data/links, and restrained translucent navigation. The custom FINN mark is an unboxed f with a detached baseline segment, echoed in the app icons. It is original vector work; Apple branding/assets are not copied.

DM Sans provides a legally distributable geometric product face with tabular data spacing. Newsreader is reserved for quotations. Headlines use direct product labels rather than rhetorical slogans. Dark selected controls, thin borders and dense financial tables provide structure; quiet elevation and modest corners distinguish interactive surfaces. No decorative hero imagery is needed in an analyst workspace.

Warnings, review badges and missing-data states are part of the hierarchy. They must remain visible even when engine telemetry is reduced. Future polish must preserve the 24-hour default, primary-source discipline, explicit units/basis and conservative comparison states.
